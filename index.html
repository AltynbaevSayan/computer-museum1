First Generation - Vacuum Tubes

Years: 1940s-1950s

The first computers used vacuum tubes. They were very large, expensive and used a lot of electricity.

Exhibit: ENIAC

Year: 1945

Generation: First Generation

Technology: Vacuum tubes

Purpose: Complex calculations

Interesting fact: ENIAC was one of the first general-purpose electronic computers.

Second Generation - Transistors

Years: 1950s-1960s

The second generation used transistors instead of vacuum tubes. Computers became smaller, faster and more reliable.

Exhibit: IBM 1401

Year: 1959

Generation: Second Generation

Technology: Transistors

Purpose: Business data processing

Interesting fact: IBM 1401 was widely used by businesses for data processing.

Third Generation - Integrated Circuits

Years: 1960s-1970s

Third generation computers used integrated circuits. They became smaller and more powerful.

Exhibit: IBM System/360

Year: 1964

Generation: Third Generation

Technology: Integrated circuits

Purpose: Business and scientific work

Interesting fact: The System/360 family was designed for many different types of computer tasks.

Fourth Generation - Microprocessors

Years: 1970s-Today

The fourth generation introduced microprocessors. Personal computers became smaller and more affordable.

Exhibit: Altair 8800

Year: 1975

Generation: Fourth Generation

Technology: Microprocessor

Purpose: Personal computing

Interesting fact: The Altair 8800 became an important early personal computer.

Fifth Generation - Artificial Intelligence

Years: Modern Era

Modern computers use artificial intelligence and machine learning. AI can recognize images, understand language and learn from data.

Exhibit: Artificial Intelligence

Generation: Fifth Generation

Technology: AI and Machine Learning

Purpose: Intelligent tasks

Interesting fact: Modern AI can work with text, images, speech and other types of information.

Computer History Timeline

1940s - First Generation - Vacuum Tubes

1950s - Second Generation - Transistors

1960s - Third Generation - Integrated Circuits

1970s - Fourth Generation - Microprocessors

Today - Fifth Generation - Artificial Intelligence

Did You Know?

  • Early computers could occupy an entire room.
  • Early computers used a lot of electricity.
  • Transistors made computers smaller and more reliable.
  • Microprocessors helped create personal computers.
  • Modern AI can understand human language.

The Future of Computing

In the future, computers may become smaller and more powerful. Artificial intelligence will become more important. People may use smart glasses instead of normal screens. Robots may help people at home and at work. Quantum computers may solve difficult problems. Brain-computer interfaces may connect people and computers. Technology will continue to change our everyday life.
